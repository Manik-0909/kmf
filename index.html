Creating a future where tick-borne diseases are understood, recognised and treated.

We’re working to build a better understanding of Australia’s tick-borne diseases by supporting research, sharing knowledge and helping bring clearer answers to those affected.

HEAR OUR MISSION

It started with ONE BITE.

Sometimes, one small bite can change everything. Karl McManus’s story sparked a movement to better understand tick-borne illness in Australia and help others avoid the uncertainty he faced. Today, his story continues to inspire research, awareness and action. Discover Karl’s story and learn how you can be part of the change.
